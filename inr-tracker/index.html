INR Tracker

Your INR results and warfarin schedule, in one place.

INR Tracker is a mobile app for people taking warfarin. It helps you keep a clear record of your INR results and follow the dosing schedule your clinician prescribed.

What you can do

  • Record INR results and see whether each one is within your target range.
  • Save the warfarin regimens your clinician gives you: daily doses, a weekly total, or a repeating pattern, matched to your tablet strengths.
  • See today's dose and your recent INR trend at a glance.
  • Get medication reminders that never show health details on the lock screen.
  • Sign in with Google or with an email and password.
Not medical advice. INR Tracker records information you and your clinician provide. It does not calculate, recommend, or change warfarin doses. Always follow your clinician's instructions, and contact them or emergency services if you are unwell.

Privacy

Your data is used only to run the app for you. It is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking. Read the full privacy policy.
